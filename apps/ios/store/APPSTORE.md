## 二、商店文案

### 繁體中文

**副標題**（30 字內）：台灣開源知識庫，離線也能讀

**宣傳文字**（170 字內）：一千多篇有出處的台灣長文，13 種語言，沒有網路也能讀、也能搜。內容每天跟網站同步。

**描述**：

Taiwan.md 是一個開源的台灣知識庫。一千多篇策展長文，從二二八、珍珠奶茶到台積電，從原住民族到金曲獎，用台灣人自己的話寫，每一篇都附出處，再翻譯成 13 種語言。

這個 App 把全部內容裝進手機。

・離線閱讀：中文、英文、日文內建在 App 裡，第一次打開就能讀；其他 10 種語言在設定裡下載
・離線搜尋：標題、摘要、全文都搜得到，查一個字也可以
・時間軸：從三萬年前的南島原鄉到今天，八個時代，每個事件連到對應的文章
・今日一篇：每天一篇長文，也能放在主畫面小工具
・收藏與閱讀進度：讀到一半的文章，下次從同一個地方接著讀
・13 種語言：同一篇文章可以直接切換語言，阿拉伯文由右到左排版
・閱讀設定：字級、深色模式；腳註點一下就看得到出處

內容以 CC BY-SA 4.0 授權，由 Taiwan.md 貢獻者共同撰寫。每篇都能在 GitHub 上看到原始檔與修改紀錄，發現錯誤可以直接回報。App 的程式碼以 MIT 授權公開。

不需要帳號，沒有廣告，不追蹤，不收集任何資料。

**關鍵字**（100 字元內）：台灣,知識庫,台灣史,歷史,文化,離線閱讀,原住民,美食,人物,教育,旅遊,Taiwan

**此版本新增內容**：第一版。

### English

**Subtitle**: Open knowledge of Taiwan, offline

**Promotional text**: 1,000+ sourced long-form articles about Taiwan in 13 languages. Read and search everything without a connection; content syncs with the website daily.

**Description**:

Taiwan.md is an open-source knowledge base about Taiwan. More than a thousand curated long-form articles, from the 228 Incident and bubble tea to TSMC, from Indigenous peoples to the Golden Melody Awards, written in Taiwanese people's own words, each with sources, and translated into 13 languages.

This app keeps all of it on your phone.

• Read offline: Chinese, English and Japanese are built in and work on first launch; download the other 10 languages in Settings
• Search offline: titles, summaries and full text, even single-character queries
• Timeline: eight eras from Austronesian origins 30,000 years ago to today, each event linked to its article
• Today's read: one long-form article a day, also as a Home Screen widget
• Bookmarks and reading progress: pick up where you left off
• 13 languages: switch any article to another language; Arabic reads right to left
• Reading options: text size, dark mode, and tap a footnote to see its source

Content is by Taiwan.md contributors under CC BY-SA 4.0. Every article's source file and edit history is public on GitHub, and anyone can report a correction. The app's code is MIT-licensed.

No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.

**Keywords**: Taiwan,history,culture,offline,reader,knowledge,Indigenous,food,travel,Taipei,Formosa,education

**What's New**: First release.

## 三、App 隱私（App Privacy）

- 是否收集資料：**否，我們不會從這個 App 收集資料**（Data Not Collected）
- 依據：App 內沒有分析、廣告或當機回報 SDK；沒有帳號；收藏、進度、設定只存本機；網路請求只抓 GitHub Release 的內容包與 taiwan.md 的圖片，不帶識別碼。
- Privacy Manifest：`App/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`（NSPrivacyTracking=false；UserDefaults CA92.1、File timestamp C617.1、Disk space E174.1）

## 六、審查備註（App Review Information → Notes，英文）

```text
Taiwan.md is an open-source, non-commercial knowledge base about Taiwan (https://taiwan.md, source: https://github.com/frank890417/taiwan-md). This app is a native reader for its 1,150+ curated, sourced articles in 13 languages.

- All content works offline. Chinese, English and Japanese are bundled in the app; feel free to test in Airplane Mode. Other languages can be downloaded in Settings > Offline packs.
- Native features: offline full-text search, a timeline of Taiwan's history, bookmarks and reading progress, a Today's Read widget, per-article language switching, and Universal Links for taiwan.md.
- Content updates: the app downloads updated content packs from the project's public GitHub Release "app-packs". Each pack is a SQLite database containing article text and HTML only; no executable code is downloaded.
- No accounts, no user-generated content, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. External links open in Safari.
- Some history articles describe violence (for example the 228 Incident and the martial law era) in text; our age rating answers reflect this.
```

不需要示範帳號。
