The sentence is in the EN translation of `knowledge/History/清朝統治時期.md`. The reader's objection is well-formed: if the Qing state ran a minimal, hands-off administration with no public education system for most of its rule, then "providing the foundation for the formation of Taiwan's later society and culture" is doing a lot of work without saying through what.

**Triage**: this is not in this shift's heal scope, for two reasons that are both structural rather than discretionary:

2. **It is a claim rewrite, not a correction.** Per [MAINTAINER-PIPELINE §1c](https://github.com/frank890417/taiwan-md/blob/main/docs/pipelines/MAINTAINER-PIPELINE.md) the maintainer seat does not change the substance of zh SSOT prose — that routes to REWRITE-PIPELINE. Replacing a sweeping "provided the foundation" with what actually carried over (land tenure and the 大租／小租 system, lineage and village organisation, temple networks, the 羅漢腳 demographic legacy, Han settlement patterns) is an EVOLVE-sized edit with its own sourcing, not a sentence patch.

Worth recording that the reader is right about the premise: the Qing administration on Taiwan was famously thin for most of two centuries (the 渡臺禁令 era, 班兵 rotation, 三年一小反 五年一大亂), and public schooling was 書院／義學 run by gentry and officials ad hoc, not a state system. So "foundation" needs to be either specified or softened — it cannot stay as a bare assertion.
