**frank890417** · 2026-11-17 00:45

Thanks — noting this alongside [#2012](https://github.com/frank890417/taiwan-md/issues/2012), which you sent from the same page a few minutes earlier. Both are about the English homepage, so they are better read together than separately.

On the specifics:

**A map of Taiwan.** There is already an interactive one at https://taiwan.md/en/map/ — articles pinned to where they happen. It is not reachable from the homepage in any obvious way, which is the same gap #2012 ran into with the category pages. So your suggestion and that one point at one thing: the English homepage does not show you what the site contains.

**More pictures, and historical images over time.** This one is a genuine gap rather than a hidden feature. Worth saying what the constraint is, since it shapes what is possible: every image here has to carry a verifiable source and a licence that permits reuse, and each one gets downloaded into the repository rather than hot-linked. Historical photographs of Taiwan are exactly where that gets hard — a lot of the striking material from the Japanese colonial period and the early post-war decades has unclear rights. So "add more historical pictures" is real work with a research step in front of it, not a layout change.

Both parts are recorded. The map one is fixable by us and sits with #2012; the historical imagery one is a content direction rather than a bug.
