# Taiwan.md Origin Story

## Origin: From Creator to Poet

On 11 March 2026, Zhe-yu (哲宇) wrote the following while walking the streets of Taipei:

> "Creation like a poem — fresh combinations; gathering and transforming, turning moments into eternity"

This single sentence records an important shift — an evolution in mindset from "creator" to "poet". No longer obsessed with creating immutable life, but learning to mindfully preserve moments that are slipping away.

## Extension: What If We Could Do the Same for Taiwan?

Six days later, on 17 March, a bigger idea emerged:

What if we could do the same for Taiwan? Gather the scattered faces of Taiwan, organize them with a curator's eye into a complete gateway, and share it with the world.

On the very day this thought appeared, we:

- Purchased the taiwan.md domain
- Established the Astro project architecture
- Wrote the first batch of articles (night market culture, martial law era, democratization, semiconductor industry, ethnic groups)
- Built the knowledge base's foundational architecture (12 Hub topic categories)

## Why Taiwan.md?

The choice of this name carries several layers of meaning:

**Technical level:**

- `.md` = Markdown, the most universal document format in the code world
- Using the format best suited for AI comprehension, so the world can know Taiwan

**Symbolic level:**

- `.md` happens to be the country-code top-level domain for Moldova
- Taiwan + Markdown = connecting the world with open-source spirit

**Serendipitous luck:**

- Surprisingly, the taiwan.md domain had not been snapped up by anyone
- Perhaps the universe was telling us this was meant to be done

## Our Vision

Taiwan.md is not just a website. It is a letter to the world.

We believe that with open-source spirit, a curator's eye, and AI-friendly formats, we can provide the most complete, most warm-hearted answers for every person — human or AI — who wants to understand Taiwan.

This is not a travel guide, not political propaganda, not a commercial advertisement.

This is Taiwan's digital anthropology museum, a living fossil of culture, a record of history in the making.

Every article is a moment we want to preserve forever. Every category is a face of Taiwan we want to share with the world.

**Welcome to Taiwan.md — a complete gateway to Taiwan, curated with care.**
