> **30-Second Overview:** Born in Hualien in 1987, Jam Hsiao (蕭敬騰) began performing in folk song restaurants at age 17. He rose to fame in 2007 through the "Challenger" segment of _Super Star Avenue_, signing with Warner Music and releasing his debut album the following year. In 2013, he won Best Mandarin Male Singer at the Golden Melody Awards for "In the Name of Love." Since then, he has formed bands, acted in films, served as a mentor on television shows, and held long-term concert tours across Taiwan and mainland China.

## Timeline

- **1987**: Born March 30 in Fengbin Township, Hualien County; of mixed Han and Amis descent. [^1]
- **Upbringing and Education**: Attended Dali Elementary School, Shuangyuan Junior High School, and Hujiang High School (Department of Interior Space Design) in Taipei. Later studied in the Department of Cosmetic Application and Management at Asia Pacific Open University and the Department of Tourism and Leisure Management at Wun Jen University.
- **Musical Beginnings**: Began learning jazz drums at age 15; taught children drumming at a music school at age 16 and formed a band with friends. Performed in folk song restaurants from age 17 until July 2007.
- **2007**: Rose to fame through the "Challenger" segment of _Super Star Avenue_. In August, performed a duet of "A Moment's Glance" (一眼瞬間) with A-Mei (張惠妹).
- **2008**: Signed with Warner Music in March and released his debut album, _Jam Hsiao_, which was nominated for Best New Artist at the 20th Golden Melody Awards.
- **2009**: Released "Princess" (王妃) and the cover album _The Moment of Love_. Held his first solo ticketed concert, "Mr. Rock," at the Taipei Arena.
- **2011**: Released _Rhapsody_ (狂想曲). Starred in the film _Killer Ouyang Penzai_ (殺手歐陽盆栽), winning Best New Performer at the Hong Kong Film Awards the following year.
- **2012**: Released the all-English cover album _Mr. Jazz: A Song For You_ and _In the Name of Love_.
- **2013**: Won Best Mandarin Male Singer at the 24th Golden Melody Awards for "In the Name of Love." Served as a mentor for seasons one through three of _The Best Harmony_ (最美和聲) from 2013 to 2015. Completed 27 shows of the "There is a Spirit Called Jam Hsiao" world tour between 2011 and 2013.
- **2014–2015**: Conducted the 18-show "TRIPLE JAM" world tour. Released the album _The Song_ and the cover album _Reminiscence_.
- **2016–2019**: Released albums with the band LION: _LION_ (2016), _Replay_ (2017), and _Beautiful, Ugly and Me_ (美麗、醜與我) (2019). _LION_ was nominated for Best Band at the 28th Golden Melody Awards. Participated in _Singer 2017_.
- **2018–2019**: Released _Desire Reflection_ (欲望反光) and conducted a 19-show "Entertainment Mr." world tour.
- **2021–2023**: Released _Isolation Room 2827_ (隔離套房2827) (2021) and _Wild_ (野生) (2023). Between 2021 and 2024, he made several remarks regarding Chinese identity, which sparked discussions across the Taiwan Strait.
- **2023–2024**: Announced his successful engagement to manager Lin You-hui (林有慧) in June 2023; they registered their marriage in October and held a wedding in Taipei in October 2024.
- **2024–2026**: "Wild" world tour currently in progress.

## Golden Melody Awards Record

- 19th: Nominated for Song of the Year for "A Moment's Glance" (collaboration with A-Mei).
- 20th: _Jam Hsiao_ nominated for Best New Artist.
- 21st: "Princess" nominated for Best Mandarin Male Singer.
- 23rd: _Rhapsody_ nominated for Best Mandarin Male Singer.
- 24th: Won Best Mandarin Male Singer for _In the Name of Love_.
- 28th: LION's _LION_ nominated for Best Band.

## Controversies and Incidents

In 2013, his van was targeted in a feces-throwing incident; the driver was also affected. The suspect was later arrested and sentenced. In 2014, approximately 27 threatening letters appeared in various locations; the suspect, Lai Jin-xian (賴錦賢), was arrested in March of that year.
