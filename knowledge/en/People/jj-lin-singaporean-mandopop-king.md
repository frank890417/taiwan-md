# JJ Lin: Born in Singapore, 1981; From "Jiangnan" to the Mandopop King of JJ20

> **30-Second Overview:** JJ Lin (林俊傑) was born on March 27, 1981, in Singapore. He stood out in the "Extraordinary Singer Training Class" organized by Hype Records in 1999, signed with Hype, and officially debuted after completing his military service.[^1] His debut album _Music Journey_ (樂行者) in 2003 sold over 1.2 million copies across Asia, followed by _Second Paradise_ (第二天堂) in 2004, which sold over 1.8 million copies.[^1] Regarding the Golden Melody Awards, he has won Best New Artist, as well as Best Mandarin Male Singer in 2014 and 2016.[^1] The JJ20 World Tour Concert series spanned 40 cities with 77 performances between 2022 and 2024, mobilizing a cumulative audience of 2.6 million.[^1]

## From Training Class to Debut

JJ Lin was born on March 27, 1981, in Singapore. In 1999, he stood out in the "Extraordinary Singer Training Class" organized by Hype Records, passed up the opportunity to attend the National University of Singapore, signed with Hype, and officially debuted after completing his military service.[^1]

## _Music Journey_ and _Second Paradise_

On April 10, 2003, his debut album _Music Journey_ (樂行者) was released, selling over 1.2 million copies across Asia. On June 4, 2004, his second album _Second Paradise_ (第二天堂) was released, with sales exceeding 1.8 million copies across Asia.[^1]

## Awards

- 15th Golden Melody Awards, Best New Artist: _Music Journey_
- 2014 (25th) Golden Melody Awards, Best Mandarin Male Singer: "Because of You" (因你而在)
- 2016 (27th) Golden Melody Awards, Best Mandarin Male Singer: "Dialogue with Myself" (和自己對話)
- 27th Golden Melody Awards, Best Composer: "A Song Not Written for Anyone" (不為誰而作的歌)[^1]

## Labels and Independence

Hype Music was his record label from 2002 to 2011, followed by Warner Music (2011 to 2021). Since 2021, his releases have been managed by his own company, JJ Music.[^1]

## JJ20 and Recent Years

The "JJ20 World Tour Concert" took place from 2022 to 2024, concluding in Chongqing from November 1 to 3, 2024. Spanning two years, the tour visited 40 cities and featured 77 performances, mobilizing a cumulative audience of 2.6 million. In 2023, he performed three consecutive nights at the Beijing Bird's Nest, attracting a total of 180,000 spectators.[^1]

In April 2024, he was diagnosed with a heart condition that requires daily medication; he disclosed this information during his final tour performance at the Beijing Bird's Nest on July 13, 2025.[^1] On November 21, 2024, the biography _Beyond the Notes: JJ Lin's 20th Anniversary_ (超越音符：林俊傑20周年) was published.[^1]
