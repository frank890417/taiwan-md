# Jody Chiang: From Beitou Nakasi to the Only Million-Selling Taiwanese Album

Jody Chiang (江蕙), born Jiang Shu-hui (江淑惠) on September 1, 1961, in Kaohsiung. At age nine, after her father acted as a guarantor for a friend and creditors came calling, her entire family moved to Taipei overnight. Her father's friend introduced her to performing at the _nakasi_ (traditional Taiwanese tea houses/cabarets) hotels and taverns in Beitou. [^1] Thirty years later, she won the Golden Melody Awards, and her album _Voices After Drinking_ (酒後的心聲) sold 1.16 million copies in Taiwan.

## From Nakasi to Million-Selling Success

She did not graduate from primary school until age 15. [^1] She debuted in 1981 with a Japanese-language album, followed by a Taiwanese-language album the next year, but neither received much attention. It was not until 1984 that "The Shore of Parting" gave her her first taste of fame; during the inaugural Golden Melody Awards in 1990, judges used her live performance of this song as a basis for evaluation. [^1]

The year 1992 marked a turning point with _Voices After Drinking_. Records show that this album sold 1.16 million copies in Taiwan—the only Taiwanese-language album to break the million mark in official records—with global sales exceeding 3 million copies. [^1] Between 2000 and 2003, she won the Best Female Dialect or Taiwanese Singer award for four consecutive years, after which she announced she would no longer compete for the award in order to encourage newcomers. [^1]

Among her representative works, "After Home" (家後), included in her 2001 self-titled album _Jody Chiang_, expresses the emotions of a wife toward her husband; it has been requested over 7 million times on KTV. At the 20th Golden Melody Awards in 2009, it ranked first in the "My Favorite Song" category. "Sound of Rain" (落雨聲), included in her 1999 album _Half Drunk, Half Sober_ (半醉半清醒), has been requested over 5 million times on KTV. [^1]

## Retirement and Comeback

The 2015 _Blessing_ tour was originally scheduled for 16 shows but was later expanded to 25. At the final show in Kaohsiung on September 13, she "sealed her microphone" (retired from performing) inside a crystal box. Wikipedia lists several reasons for her retirement: chronic vertigo, her demand for perfection in performances, the passing of Feng Fei-fei (鳳飛飛), who was originally meant to perform alongside her, and the pressures of piracy and the decline of the recording industry. [^1]

On September 10, 2024, she publicly explained that her health had undergone significant changes on the eve of her 2015 concerts; after medical examination, she was diagnosed with cancer and underwent long-term treatment. The entry does not specify the type of cancer. [^1] On October 5, she performed at the National Day Gala at the Taipei Dome, marking her official comeback. [^1]
