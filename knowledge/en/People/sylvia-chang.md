# Sylvia Chang: Three-time Golden Horse Best Actress and Director of Sixteen Works

Sylvia Chang (張艾嘉) was born in 1953. She became a radio DJ at age sixteen, a singer and host at seventeen, and made her film debut at nineteen. She has won the Golden Horse Award for Best Actress three times—in 1981, 1986, and 2022—with a forty-one-year gap between her second and third wins. During this time, she also directed sixteen works, ranging from her directorial debut _One Moment in Time_ (某年某月某一天) in 1981 to _There Is She_ (世間有她) in 2022.[^1]

## First a Singer and Host, Then an Actor

Her career did not begin on a film set. According to Wikipedia, she was a radio DJ at 16 and a singer/host at 17; her first film, _Flying Tiger_, did not arrive until 1972.[^1] Her filmography in the 1970s was extensive, including _Plum Blossom_ (梅花), _Blue Sky_ (碧雲天), and _The Eighty Heroes_ (八百壯士) in 1976. Notably, _Blue Sky_ earned her her first Golden Horse Award for Best Supporting Actress.[^1]

Her musical career has remained continuous. Her discography includes albums such as _Childhood_ (童年), _Perhaps_ (也許), _Busy and Blind_ (忙與盲), _Do You Love Me?_ (你愛我嗎), and _The Price of Love_ (愛的代價). The latter was released by Rock Records in 1992, with lyrics and composition by Jonathan Lee; the earliest recorded version is credited to Chang. This song was later covered by Jonathan Lee in his 1994 album _Jonathan Lee's Music Journey: Not Letting Go_ and by Gigi Leung (梁詠琪) in her 2000 album. The song also served as the theme for the film _Three Summers_ (三個夏天).[^2]

## 1981 and 1986: Two Best Actress Wins, Two Different Roles

In 1981, she won Best Actress at the 18th Golden Horse Awards for her role as Xu Yi-ting in _My Grandpa_. That same year, she directed her first film, _One Moment in Time_.[^1] The entry also notes that she took over to complete this film after Tu Chung-hsun (屠忠訓) left it unfinished in 1979, though the work is officially dated 1981, creating a discrepancy in the years.[^1]

Her 1986 achievement with _Love Education_ was more comprehensive: she played the role of Bai Yun while also serving as screenwriter and director. The film was nominated for Best Feature Film, Best Director, Best Original Screenplay, and Best Original Film Song at the 23rd Golden Horse Awards, and she won Best Actress.[^1]

## Directorial Works and Golden Horse Nominations

She has sixteen credited directorial works: _One Moment in Time_ (1981), _Love Education_ (1986), _Yellow Story_ (1987), _Sasha Jiajia Stands Up_ (莎莎嘉嘉站起來, 1991), _Dreaming of the Moment_ (夢醒時分, 1992), _New Coexistence_ (新同居時代, 1994), _Young Girl, Little Fisherman_ (1995), _Not Going Home Today_ (今天不回家, 1996), _Heartbeat_ (心動, 1999), _Wanting to Fly_ (想飛, 2002), _20 30 40_ (2004), _A Good Father_ (一個好爸爸, 2008), the segment "Twilight of the Gods" in _10+10_ (2011), _Nian Nian_ (念念, 2015), _Bulbul Propeller_ (2017), and _There Is She_ (2022).[^1]

Her Golden Horse records are twofold. As a director or screenwriter, she received nominations for _Love Education_, _Young Girl, Little Fisherman_, _Not Going Home Today_, and _Heartbeat_ (Best Feature Film); she was nominated for Best Director for _A Good Father_ in 2008, and for both Best Director and Best Original Screenplay for _Bulbul Propeller_ in 2017. As an actress, she has received twelve nominations or awards between 1976 and 2024, including three wins for Best Actress and one win for Best Supporting Actress.[^1]

She has also won screenwriting awards in Hong Kong: Best Screenwriter at the Hong Kong Film Awards for _Heartbeat_ (2000) and _Bulbul Propeller_ (2018); Best Screenwriter at the Hong Kong Film Critics Society Awards for _Nian Nian_ (2016); and Best Director at the same society for _Bulbul Propeller_ (2018).[^1]

## 2022: The Third Best Actress Win

In 2022, she won her third Golden Horse Award for Best Actress for _Love in the Night_, thirty-six years after her second win in 1986.[^1] Subsequently, in 2024, she was nominated for Best Actress at the 61st Golden Horse Awards for _Daughter's Daughter_. In 2025, she received a Lifetime Achievement Award at the Far East Film Festival and the Camellia Award at the Busan International Film Festival.[^1]

She is also an essayist, having published _Lightly Written_ (輕描淡寫, 2015) and _Daughter_ (女兒, 2024).[^1]
