> **30-Second Overview:** VH (Vast & Hazy) is a Taiwanese indie duo. Members Ka-Ka (Yen Ching-hsuan) and Yi-chi (Lin Yi-chi) formed in 2011 at the Tamkang Golden Bell Awards and have been nominated for Best Vocal Group at the Golden Melody Awards three times (2019, 2022, 2025). Using a creative philosophy of wrapping human observations in gentle vocals, they rose against the tide of the political rock-dominated indie scene of the 2010s, earning the spontaneous nickname "outlet band" from fans. Following their _Moving Forward_ annual special in April 2026, Yi-chi announced a shift to a primarily behind-the-scenes role, entering a new phase centered around Ka-Ka.

On April 10, 2026, SUB LIVE in Taipei hosted the RE: VH annual special, _Moving Forward_. After the performance, Yi-chi made an announcement: from now on, his role will shift primarily to behind-the-scenes work, and he will no longer perform on stage at every show. [^7]

Fifteen years of "moving forward" have taken on a new shape.

### The Intersection of Two Guitar Clubs

In 2008, Ka-Ka (Yen Ching-hsuan) was a high school junior, and Yi-chi (Lin Yi-chi) was a senior. Both were members of guitar clubs at different high schools in Taipei and missed each other during a five-school joint exhibition—until they officially met during their university interviews on the same day in their senior year.

A few years later, they found themselves in the same music circles at Tamkang University. In 2011, accompanied by drummer Bai-hu, they participated in the 23rd Tamkang Golden Bell Awards with the original song 〈yet,〉, winning first place for Composition, as well as Best Lyrics and Best Arrangement. [^1] At that time, even their band name had not been finalized—but this competition record marks the earliest public existence of Vast & Hazy.

### A Necessary Disappearance

At the end of 2013, bassist Xiao-han (Lü Yi-chan) joined the group. Then, in 2014, the Vast & Hazy lineup dispersed once again—after graduation, members took on other life responsibilities; Bai-hu became a recording engineer for Huang Chieh-wei, while Ka-Ka and Yi-chi lived their own lives outside of music for a period.

This hiatus lasted until 2017, when Yi-chi restarted the project. The two decided to continue as a duo.

That same year, they released the EP _Substandard Secrets_, which included 〈[Between with the Waves](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10DHUqSVu6I)〉—which was later nominated for Best Music Video at the 29th Golden Melody Awards. This song served as the first public marker of the duo-format Vast & Hazy and the starting point for establishing their sonic direction. [^2]

### _I'm Not OK_: Turning "My" Anxiety into "Our" Anxiety

In June 2018, their first full-length album, _I'm Not OK_ (求救訊號), was released.

The title is direct, but the presentation is not a scream. 〈I'm Not OK〉, 〈Malfunction〉, 〈Indiscriminate Harm〉, 〈I Want to Be You〉—every song speaks of brokenness, but does so quietly. That year, two release shows at Legacy Taipei sold out; in 2019, they took this album on a 19-city tour across Taiwan, covering 16 administrative districts, all of which were sold out. [^3]

This album earned them a nomination for Best Vocal Group at the 30th Golden Melody Awards.

Ka-Ka spoke about the origins of the album at the time:

> "As we gradually lose our youthful fearlessness, insecurity—doubting ourselves and the world—slips in through the cracks. Not every dream is destined to come true; the version of ourselves we most want to become often can only hide behind us like a shadow." [^1]

In the context of Taiwan during 2018–2019—amidst waves of workplace anxiety, housing pressure, and generational burnout—these words resonated deeply with many.

### "Hoping Listeners Interpret It Into Different Shapes"

Ka-Ka's creative philosophy is rooted in a refusal to be overly explicit.

She enjoys reading novels that explore human nature and deliberately avoids romance as her primary theme: "To me, love is not everything; I draw inspiration from the flow of emotion between people, and from reflections on facing oneself and empathizing with others." [^5] She writes about anxiety, loss, and the unnamable feelings of modern people.

However, she does not wish to dictate the interpretation:

> "I hope to give the listeners more agency. Whether it is the lyrics or the promotional copy for a song, I don't want to state things too definitively; I hope listeners interpret them into their own different shapes. It's like a movie—if the plot is too direct and simple, it loses its charm and the space to develop in one's mind." [^1]

This design turns VH's songs into emotional containers: they can hold heartbreak, workplace burnout, or any indefinable discomfort. Fans later spontaneously gave them a title: "the outlet band."

### _The Great Beyond_: A Memorandum for the Apocalypse

On December 30, 2021, their second album, _The Great Beyond_ (文明), was released.

This is a concept album framed as "apocalyptic files," where each song is accompanied by an "exhibit card" to serve as a clue for interpretation. The music video for 〈[The Great Beyond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXcuZilZjPw)〉 uses visuals to concretize this framework: the dialectic between rules and escape.

This album was nominated for Best Vocal Group at the 33rd Golden Melody Awards.

In a later interview, Yi-chi said:

> "Some things aren't there during the recording; they gradually take shape after performing live several times. More new things will grow out of that." [^7]

This is a microcosm of Vast & Hazy's entire creative logic: the final version is the starting point, not the destination.

### _5S_ and the New VH Name

In May 2024, their third album, _5S_ (五常法則), was released. The conceptual framework borrows from the Japanese 5S methodology used in corporate management—but in reverse: using the language of organization and tidiness to describe humanity's struggle and escape from order.

In February 2025, they officially simplified their name to **VH**, simultaneously releasing the live compilation _LIVE Arr. Collection_—seven songs spanning 15 years of creativity. These are live versions, the physical manifestation of what Yi-chi calls "the new things that grow after playing dozens of shows." [^4]

### Ka-Ka's Era of Autonomy

In July 2025, Ka-Ka released the single 〈[The Necessary Vulnerability and Unnecessary Silence Before Becoming Art](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TW266wakB90)〉.

What makes this song special is that the lyrics, composition, arrangement, and production were all completed by her alone—it was her first public appearance as an all-around producer. [^6] Her manifesto for the song stated:

> "As a creator who has long explored selfhood and communicated with the world through music and text, I write this non-critical resistance to face questioning voices arising from differences in stance or aesthetics." [^6]

"Non-critical resistance"—in the context of Taiwan in 2025, this phrase carries weight. In an opinion market filled with competitions for volume and visibility, choosing to resist through gentleness is a stance in itself.

### The Shape of Moving Forward

When Yi-chi announced his shift behind the scenes, he said: "I'm still here; I just want to focus more on making VH better." [^7]

Over fifteen years, Vast & Hazy has undergone three identity shifts: the formation of a multi-member group in 2011, the hiatus and return as a duo from 2014–2017, and Yi-chi's withdrawal from the front stage in 2026. Each time was a calibration of direction—moving toward the same destination, but via a different path.

The title "outlet band" was given by fans, not self-proclaimed by them. Interestingly, it is precisely because of this that the name is so accurate. In an era where many bands use their sound to tell the audience how to think, Vast & Hazy's choice is: to give you an outlet, and let you decide where to go.
