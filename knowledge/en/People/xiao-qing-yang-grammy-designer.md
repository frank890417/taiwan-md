# Hsiao Ching-yang: Bringing Taiwanese Design to the World Stage

> **30-Second Overview:** Born in Xindian in 1966 and graduated from Fu-Hsing Vocational High School, Hsiao Ching-yang is one of Taiwan's most internationally visible visual designers. He received his first Grammy nomination for Best Recording Package in 2005 and has since accumulated seven nominations. At the 65th Annual Grammy Awards in 2023, the album _Beginningless Beginning_, co-designed with his daughter Hsiao Chun-tien, finally won the Best Recording Package award on its seventh nomination. Following Li Cheng-han and Yu Wei in 2022, this victory marked two consecutive years of wins for Taiwanese designers in this category.

## Background and Design Inspiration

Hsiao Ching-yang was born on February 26, 1966, in Xindian, Taipei County (now Xindian District, New Taipei City). During his youth, he enrolled in the Fine Arts Department at Fu-Hsing Vocational High School, where he received rigorous foundational training in core visual design skills such as color, composition, and printing. Fu-Hsing Vocational High School has long been a cradle for artistic and design talent in Taiwan, and its environment nurtured his intuition for visual language. [^1]

In the 1980s, as Taiwan's record industry was flourishing, Hsiao developed a strong visual fascination with album covers from a young age and began exploring the intersection of music and design. His perception of local cultural visual elements, combined with his intuition for musical emotion, gradually formed his signature design language.

## Entering the Field of Album Design

In the early 1990s, as Taiwan's Mandopop industry entered its golden age, Hsiao began systematically dedicating himself to album packaging design. He designed works for musicians such as [Chang Yu-sheng](/en/people/chang-yu-sheng-singer/) and [Lim Giong](/en/people/lim-giong/), gradually building a reputation within the music circle. This also established a working method distinct from commercial designers: he engages deeply in the musical creative process, communicating extensively with musicians to understand the spiritual core of a work before translating it into visual language. [^2]

He excels at drawing inspiration from local Taiwanese culture; temple carvings, folk totems, and natural landscapes are all transformed through his hands into a design language capable of cross-cultural communication. This path—starting from the local to reach the international—later became his most recognizable creative hallmark.

## 2005: The First Grammy Nomination

In 2005, Hsiao Ching-yang became the first Taiwanese designer to be nominated for Best Recording Package at the 47th Grammy Awards for a design of a Taiwanese music album. [^3]

This nomination marked the first time the Taiwanese design community appeared on a Grammy list. His design language—rooted in local Taiwanese culture but presented through modern composition—offered a distinct aesthetic perspective to the Western-dominated judging panel, helping him gain recognition within the international design community. Through this, the energy of Taiwanese cultural design left its first mark on the Grammy roster.

## Seven Nominations and the 2023 Victory

From 2005 to 2023, Hsiao Ching-yang accumulated a total of seven Grammy nominations for Best Recording Package across various Taiwanese musical works, responding to each piece of music with a completely different visual strategy. While his first six nominations did not result in a win, the process itself became a significant cultural event watched closely by the Taiwanese design community. [^4]

On February 5, 2023, at the 65th Annual Grammy Awards, the soundtrack album for the short film trilogy _The Formosan Trail_ (淡蘭古道三部曲), titled _Beginningless Beginning_, co-designed by Hsiao and his daughter Hsiao Chun-tien, won the Best Recording Package award. After waiting 18 years since his first nomination, his seventh attempt finally yielded success. The previous year, Li Cheng-han and Yu Wei had secured Taiwan's first Grammy in this same category with the Sambuay album _Bage Lang_ (八歌浪). Hsiao's win ensured that Taiwanese designers won in this category for two consecutive years. This cross-generational collaboration between father and daughter also added a deeper sense of legacy to the achievement. [^5]

## Design Philosophy

Hsiao Ching-yang's design philosophy revolves around "Roots in Taiwan, Flowers Blooming Worldwide"—the idea that creators should dig deeply into local cultural resources while ensuring their expression is intelligible to an international audience. This attitude allows his work to maintain its identity in a globalized environment and provides a pathway for Taiwan's local visual language to be exported. Rather than imitating mainstream Western design languages, he brings temples, traditional crafts, and the island's nature into the view of international jurors.

Beyond commercial commissions, his studio occasionally returns to the sites of traditional crafts to collaborate with veteran artisans, integrating traditional techniques into a modern design context. He also occasionally lectures at universities, passing on this creative philosophy of "speaking through one's own roots" to the next generation of designers. [^6]

## Significance for the Taiwanese Design Community

Hsiao Ching-yang's Grammy win demonstrates a path for the Taiwanese design community: there is no need to avoid local culture; Taiwan's visual language can hold its ground in international competitions. His achievement inspires subsequent designers to excavate local resources rather than viewing them as obstacles to entering the international market. His long-standing domestic recognition, including awards for Best Album Packaging at the Golden Melody Awards, provides a local context for his design prowess. [^7]
