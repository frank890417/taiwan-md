In Taiwan's alleyways, perhaps it is a steaming bowl of _niurou mian_ (beef noodle soup), or the rising and falling vendor calls in an old market — these seemingly ordinary daily moments are the most genuine memory fragments constituting Taiwan's cultural fabric. Yet the torrent of time is merciless; these precious vernacular memories are rapidly disappearing with the passing of elders and the alteration of landscapes. To address this, the Ministry of Culture launched the "National Cultural Memory Bank" project in 2017. This is not merely a digital archive platform, but a warm social engineering project wrestling with time, aiming to harness technology so that Taiwan's cultural memories can be seen, preserved, and revitalized. [^3]

📝 Curator's Note: The value of the National Cultural Memory Bank lies in its transformation of cold digital data into warm stories, allowing everyone to find resonance within, and even shift from being an "observer" to a "co-creator of memory."

## National Cultural Memory Bank: From Archive to Co-creation — A Cultural Bank

The core concept of the National Cultural Memory Bank is to integrate Taiwan's cultural assets scattered across regions through a systematic "survey, collect, store, retrieve, use" mechanism. [^8] It pools the strengths of county and city governments, civil organizations, central cross-ministerial agencies, and the 12 Ministry of Education-affiliated museums, jointly treasuring the vernacular life portraits under Taiwan's grand era. [^3] This is a "national cultural bank" concept — treating cultural memory as a circulating, reusable treasure, not merely a static collection locked in a vault.

### 2.0 Transformation: More User-friendly, Thematic, International

With the evolution of digital technology, the National Cultural Memory Bank has recently promoted a 2.0 transformation project with three goals: "platform user-friendliness, content thematization, readership internationalization." [^10] This means the platform not only optimized search interfaces and license labeling, but also, through professional historical workers' curation, transformed content into more engaging thematic stories, making it easier for the public to access Taiwan's cultural depth. For instance, through NLP (natural language processing) technology, search is no longer mere keyword matching but can understand users' exploratory intent. [^9]

## Revitalizing Memory: From "Time Travel Agency" to "Food Memory"

The charm of the National Cultural Memory Bank lies in how it revitalizes these digitized memories, making them not just archives but cultural materials that can be experienced and re-created. Among these, the "Time Travel Agency" is an innovative online curation platform encouraging users to become curators, sharing the Taiwan stories they know. [^7] Through thematic curation — such as "Taiwan Sweet: The Island's Sweet Flavors" or "Trans-temporal Island Table" — the Memory Bank combines food culture with historical context, letting readers experience Taiwan's transformation through their taste buds. [^12] [^14]

These cases demonstrate how the Memory Bank has transformed from a mere database into a vibrant cultural co-creation field. It not only provides materials but also sparks creators' inspiration, allowing these memories to return to public view in new forms — images, text, artworks, and more. [^13]

## Challenges and Prospects: Balancing Copyright and Data Openness

However, in promoting data openness and revitalization, the National Cultural Memory Bank also faces complex copyright issues. [^1] [^4] Striking a balance between encouraging utilization and protecting original creators' rights is a direction the Memory Bank continues to pursue. Through clear license labeling and CC licensing articles, the platform attempts to guide users toward fair use, emphasizing that data openness is not merely a technical issue but the practice of a cultural sharing philosophy. [^2] [^6]

In the future, the National Cultural Memory Bank will continue to deepen its concept of "individual micro-narratives converging into a national grand history," [^11] encouraging more citizens to participate in memory collection and sharing. Through continuous optimization and innovation, it will become a sustainably developing cultural ecosystem, allowing Taiwan's cultural DNA to continue evolving in the digital age and telling Taiwan's unique and rich stories to the world.
